# Personal Identification Information (PII):
- EU GDPR Art. 4(1), Art 4(5) and Recital 26
-CJEU C-413/23 P EDPS v SRB (2025)
  - Personal data which have undergone pseudonymisation which could be attributed to a natural person by the use of additional information, should
  be considered to be information on an identifiable natural person. To determine whether a natural person is identifiable, account should be taken of all the means reasonably likely to be used, such as singling out, either by the controller or by another person, to identify the natural person directly or indirectly.
- e-Privacy Directive (Directive 2002/58/EC) Article 9(1)
    - Where location data other than traffic data, relating to users or subscribers of public communications networks or publicly available electronic communications services, can be processed, such data may only be processed when they are made anonymous, or with the consent of the users or subscribers to the extent and for the duration necessary for the provision of a value added service.
